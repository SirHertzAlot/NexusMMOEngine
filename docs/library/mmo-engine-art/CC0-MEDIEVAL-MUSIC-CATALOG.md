## ⚠️ Ambiguous — needs verification before use

1. **alkakrab — "Medieval Free Soundtrack" (8 Medieval Tracks For Your Game), itch.io** — Labeled "No Copyright" / "free". itch.io does not enforce formal license metadata; "No Copyright" is the seller's own wording, NOT a CC0 dedication. The direct project page URL was not recoverable from the profile listing page. **Do not treat as CC0** until the project page's license text is read. (alkakrab also publishes "8 Dark Fantasy Boss Battle Tracks," pirate packs, etc. — same caveat applies.)
2. **SuloSounds — "Free 25 RPG Tracks For Your Games!", itch.io** — itch.io listing text states "License: Creative Commons CC0." Claim appears in itch's own listing text (moderate confidence), but the full license file was not inspected. Verify on the project page before shipping.
3. **Frostbite Medieval Soundtrack (OpenGameArt)** — The album page is tagged **CC-BY 3.0**, and per the album description several tracks are derivatives of CC-BY 3.0 works (HitCtrl, Yubatake) requiring attribution. A few component tracks are individually CC0 (The Old Tower Inn, Castle, Original Hymn — listed above separately). **The album as a whole is NOT CC0**; use only the individually-verified CC0 component tracks.

## ❌ Explicitly NOT CC0 (do not use under CC0 terms)

- **Kevin MacLeod / incompetech.com** — CC-BY 4.0 (attribution required). The standard catalog is NOT CC0. (Only the separate freepd.com catalog is public domain — see above.) Usable as a CC-BY alternative with credit, not as CC0.
- **Pixabay Music** — Pixabay Content License, NOT CC0. Free commercial use, no attribution required, but prohibits standalone redistribution and is revocable/territorially limited. Fails the CC0 requirement. (Pre-2019-01-09 uploads were CC0, but per-track upload dates are not reliably verifiable.)
- **Uppbeat (free tier)** — attribution REQUIRED, monthly download cap, and per Uppbeat's own FAQ the free license covers non-commercial content only. NOT CC0.
- **Free Music Archive (general)** — mixed licenses; most tracks are CC-BY-NC or similar. Only the public-domain-filtered tracks listed above qualify.
- **GameDev Market / MonsterONE / TemplateMonster medieval packs** — paid commercial licenses, not CC0.

## Verification limits (honest statement)

- OGA license tags are self-declared by uploaders. I verified the tag on each OGA page directly, and cross-checked the most-used tracks against CREDITS.md files of shipped open-source games that did their own diligence — but I did not contact composers.
- "Uploader is the composer" risk: RandomMind, cynicmusic, Bobjt, and Kevin MacLeod (freepd) all present as the original composers with consistent multi-year catalogs — low risk. Single-track uploaders (Le Mandrill, Tozan, Eldritch Grim, pauliuw) carry the standard OGA residual risk.
- freepd.com CC0 status rests on MacLeod's own public statements ("released into the public domain (CC Zero)") plus the SoundSafari CC0-1.0 mirror; I did not fetch a formal dedication deed from freepd.com itself.
- FMA public-domain status rests on FMA's own filter tagging; FMA's "public domain" includes both CC0 dedications and expired-copyright works.
- itch.io items could not be license-verified (see Ambiguous section) — itch.io has no license-metadata API and project pages require individual inspection.
- Recommendation: before shipping, re-verify the 5–10 tracks actually selected, keep a CREDITS.md with source URLs even though attribution isn't legally required (it documents diligence), and prefer the multi-cross-checked tracks (Old Tower Inn, cynicmusic trio, freepd staples) for the most prominent placements.
