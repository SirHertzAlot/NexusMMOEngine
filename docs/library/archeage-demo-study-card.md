# ArcheAge Chronicles Demo — 20-Minute Study Card

Demo window: **Oct 19, 1pm EDT → Oct 26** (Steam Next Fest). For if you dip in —
no pressure either way. Each item below maps to one of your engine's open design
questions; the empty ArcheAge section in
`~/workspace/your_files/mmo-engine-gameplay/COMBAT-ABILITIES.md` is where any of
it goes if you want it filed.

## 1. Horizontal progression (vs your M5 ability system)
Weapon trait points come from **Coilstones guarded by world bosses**, not levels.
Each weapon has its own trait tree: stat nodes, new skills, skill modifiers
(their example: a Greatsword branch widening a skill into a two-hit swing).
Question to bring home: how does a build that belongs to explorers who beat hard
bosses *feel* next to a level-granted ability kit? Direct contrast material for
your consensual-PvP ability design.

## 2. PvP opt-in + incentive (vs your DuelSystem)
~100-player zone channels, separate standard vs PvP channels, opt-in through a
**Nui Statue**, and a **10–15% monster XP bonus** in PvP channels as the carrot.
This is a shipped opt-in + incentive pattern — watch whether the bonus feels like
a real reason to flag, or background noise.

## 3. Boss telegraphs (vs your TelegraphSystem)
Your telegraphs are server-authoritative danger zones. Watch theirs: read-time,
shape language, how fair a first-time pull feels. Note anything their presentation
does that yours doesn't — steal the read, not the code.

## 4. Throne of Hinnon (vs your dungeon module)
A high-level dungeon placed deliberately *outside* the demo's progression, entered
via a hidden key stashed somewhere hard to find. A "dungeon out of reach" as a
design device — worth 30 seconds of thought on whether your dungeon module wants
an equivalent teaser affordance.

## 5. Loadout texture
Four classes, **two weapons each**. Notice how much variety two weapon sets per
class actually produce in practice — relevant to how deep your weapon/trait
combinations need to be before they feel distinct.

## 6. Economy-adjacent details
One-open-per-character treasure chests, roaming field bosses dropping gear found
nowhere else, Nui Tears traded at Nui Statues for max HP, Hersander's Tower
riddle-offerings unlocking timed leaderboard boss fights. Skim for anything that
rhymes with your needs/economy thinking; most of it is flavor, one of them might
be a mechanic.
